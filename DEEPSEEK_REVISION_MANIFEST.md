# DeepSeek Revision Manifest

The supplied DeepSeek export was used as the recovery source for the uncommitted design/function revisions made before the later client palette request.

## Shop revisions restored

- Responsive, full-width checkout rather than a centered/max-width layout.
- Revised cart to visually continue the checkout experience.
- Shop collection/grid revisions and category navigation behavior.
- Reusable product cards and product detail improvements, including visible colour swatches and Buy Now flow.
- Wishlist revisions, including empty state and header count behavior.
- Footer refinements and removal of the excessive vertical gap.
- Delivery, Returns, Portfolio, FAQ and Track Order page revisions.
- Login, registration, forgot-password and account-area refinements.
- Commerce order snapshots so created orders retain their purchased line items.
- Checkout-success order-summary flow.

## Admin revisions restored

- Redesigned admin dashboard.
- Revised customers, shipments and analytics screens.
- Product management prototype with add/edit/delete actions.
- Product form spacing/padding corrections.
- Product image URL field and preview support.
- Redesigned orders listing with status filters.
- Dedicated order-detail route.
- Dispatch-to-store action and store assignment state.

## Later palette retained

The transcript predates the client's colour-palette change. Its layout and feature revisions were restored, but the project keeps the subsequently approved palette:

- Deep Green `#0E2B26`
- Sage Green `#8CA78B`
- Warm Beige `#E8DFCF`
- Soft Cream `#FAF7F2`
- Gold `#D4AF37`
- Charcoal `#1F2321`
