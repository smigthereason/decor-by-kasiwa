# Design Notes

## Reference interpretation

The supplied ecommerce/interior reference is being translated into Decor by Kasiwa through:

- warm off-white surfaces rather than stark white
- large central KASIWA wordmark as a temporary brand device
- small caption typography and precise micro-labels
- floating image blocks around the hero wordmark
- a large immersive interior scene immediately after the hero
- translucent project/product callout cards
- horizontal category rails
- strong contrast between ivory and deep charcoal/green
- fewer conventional ecommerce cards above the fold

## Brand system

- Canvas: `#EFEAE2`
- Paper: `#F7F3ED`
- Ink: `#1C1C19`
- Forest: `#263B31`
- Sand: `#C8BBA8`
- Gold accent: `#9B825B`

Typography uses Manrope for the first pass to retain the clean contemporary feel seen in the reference.

## UX principle

Decor by Kasiwa should not feel like a furniture warehouse.

The hierarchy is:

1. Transformation
2. Portfolio / credibility
3. Services
4. Shop
5. Consultation

Commerce is present, but the studio's design expertise leads the experience.
